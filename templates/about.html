About us

We believe every book deserves to be remembered.

Bookshelf started in 2024 as a side project between a few friends who kept losing track of what they had read. Notes lived in phone memos, ratings in forgotten spreadsheets, and the best recommendations disappeared in group chats. We wanted one calm, beautiful place to keep every book we had loved, along with why we loved it, and we couldn't find one that didn't feel like a store or a social network. So we built our own.

Today Bookshelf is a small, independent team of readers, designers and engineers. We make tools for people who read slowly and remember deliberately. There are no ads, no algorithms pushing bestsellers, and no streaks to keep up. It's just your library, written in your own words. We think a good reading journal should feel like the margins of a well-loved paperback: personal, a little messy, and something you'll want to come back to years from now.
